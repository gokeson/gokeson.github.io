∞ 02 / FEATURED SOFTWARE

CtGAP

Open-source genome assembly, strain typing and phylogenomics for Chlamydia trachomatis.

FEATURED · PEER-REVIEWED SOFTWARE

From sequencing reads to interpretable pathogen genomes.

My contributions to CtGAP span its initial development through to CtGAP 2.0, incorporating advances in genome assembly, strain characterisation, and comparative genomics. I continue to actively maintain and improve the pipeline. The updated framework streamlines installation and command-line use, automates assembly-quality assessment and selection, and integrates chromosome and plasmid typing with phylogenomics.
